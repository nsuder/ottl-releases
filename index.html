Ottl is native dictation for the Mac. Your words land in whatever app you're typing in. A small bar on screen shows exactly when the microphone is on.

Download for Mac
Apple silicon and Intel · macOS 14 Sonoma or later · signed and notarized by Apple

Install

  1. Open the downloaded .dmg and drag Ottl into Applications.
  2. Open Ottl from Applications and click Enable dictation in its window. macOS then asks about the microphone and speech recognition. Allow both. (Clicked Don't Allow? The same button then reads Open System Settings… and takes you to the right place.)
  3. Ottl's window asks for Accessibility, which it needs to type into other apps. System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility → turn on Ottl, then quit and reopen Ottl.
  4. Put the cursor in any text field, hold fn, speak, release. Double‑tap fn to keep listening hands‑free; tap once to stop.
  5. If pressing fn also opens the emoji picker, switches your keyboard, or starts Apple's own dictation, set System Settings → Keyboard → Press 🌐 key to → Do Nothing, and turn off Apple Dictation's “Press 🌐 twice” shortcut.
  6. Highlight text and hold Control + fn to rewrite it with a spoken instruction.

Early access: bring your keys

This is an early build for invited testers. Ottl's best results come from two cloud services, and for now you connect your own accounts: an AssemblyAI key for speech and a Groq key for cleanup and rewriting. Paste them in Ottl → Settings → Cloud, turn cloud services on, and click Test to check the AssemblyAI key. If you choose Groq Whisper as the speech provider, the Groq key alone is enough. Keys stay in your Mac's Keychain. Without keys, Ottl uses Apple's on‑device recognition. Ottl understands US English only for now.

Privacy

Ottl records only while you hold the key or have started listening, and the bar on screen is visible whenever the microphone is on. Your voice and text never leave your Mac unless you turn cloud services on, and the window always shows which mode is active. Once a day Ottl asks this site whether a newer version exists; nothing else is sent, and you can turn it off in Settings → General. No audio files, no transcript logs, no analytics. Provider keys live only in the macOS Keychain.

Report a problem

Open an issue with one line about what happened, plus the Last recording and Last delivery lines from Ottl → Settings → Diagnostics. Those lines contain no text you dictated. Issues are public, so leave out anything private.
