Ottl is native dictation for the Mac. Your words land in whatever app you're typing in. A small axolotl shows you exactly when the microphone is on.

Download for Mac
Apple silicon and Intel · macOS 14 Sonoma or later · signed and notarized by Apple

Install

  1. Open the downloaded .dmg and drag Ottl into Applications.
  2. Open Ottl from Applications. macOS asks about the microphone and speech recognition. Allow both.
  3. Ottl's window asks for Accessibility, which it needs to type into other apps. System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility → turn on Ottl, then quit and reopen Ottl.
  4. Put the cursor in any text field, hold fn, speak, release. Double‑tap fn to keep listening hands‑free; tap once to stop.
  5. Highlight text and hold Control + fn to rewrite it with a spoken instruction.

Early access: bring your keys

This is an early build for invited testers. Ottl's best results come from two cloud services, and for now you connect your own accounts: an AssemblyAI key for speech and a Groq key for cleanup and rewriting. Paste both in Ottl → Settings and click Test cloud connection. Keys stay in your Mac's Keychain. Without keys, Ottl uses Apple's on‑device recognition.

Privacy

Ottl records only while you hold the key or have started listening, and the axolotl is visible whenever the microphone is on. Nothing leaves your Mac unless you turn cloud services on, and the window always shows which mode is active. No audio files, no transcript logs, no analytics. Provider keys live only in the macOS Keychain.

Report a problem

Ottl's window → Settings → copy the Last delivery attempt and Last recording lines and send them to Nic with one line about what happened. They contain no text you dictated.
